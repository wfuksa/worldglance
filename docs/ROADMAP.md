# Worldglance Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction rather than a rigid contract.

Milestones may change when development teaches us something important.

## 0.1 — Earth prototype

Goal:

Prove that the core Worldglance Earth experience can work.

Build:

- basic website
- 3D Earth
- smooth rotation
- zoom
- basic camera controls
- initial land/ocean appearance
- atmosphere/lighting where practical
- acceptable desktop performance

Success means:

Opening Worldglance already feels like the beginning of the real product.

Do not add unrelated systems.

---

## 0.2 — Visual identity and camera

Improve:

- Earth styling
- colors
- lighting
- atmosphere
- camera movement
- transitions between scales
- interaction feel
- basic UI shell

Goal:

Make moving around Earth feel polished.

---

## 0.3 — Geographic world

Introduce useful geographic detail such as:

- countries
- cities
- major roads
- labels
- geographic hierarchy
- progressive detail

Goal:

The globe begins to become an explorable world.

---

## 0.4 — Cities and buildings

Develop the local-scale appearance:

- stylized buildings
- roads
- city structure
- level-of-detail behavior
- city performance

Goal:

Zooming toward cities should reveal meaningful detail without destroying performance.

---

## 0.5 — Living ground activity

Introduce controlled simulation of activity such as:

- cars
- road movement
- other useful city motion

Goal:

Cities begin to feel alive.

Simulation should be bounded to relevant visible areas.

---

## 0.6 — Global transport

Introduce:

- aircraft
- ships

Explore where real data, modelled movement and simulation make sense.

Goal:

Movement becomes visible at regional and global scale.

---

## 0.7 — Environment

Introduce selected environmental systems:

- clouds
- weather
- precipitation/storm visualization
- day/night improvements
- other atmospheric systems where useful

Goal:

The Earth should feel dynamic even when the user is not focused on cities.

---

## 0.8 — Events and information

Introduce selected world events and contextual information.

Possible examples:

- earthquakes
- storms
- fires
- selected significant events

Also develop:

- selection
- contextual information
- data-truth labels
- useful timestamps/freshness

Goal:

Worldglance becomes informative without becoming cluttered.

---

## 0.9 — Product polish

Focus on:

- search
- navigation
- responsive layout
- loading states
- error handling
- accessibility
- performance
- consistency
- visual polish
- browser compatibility
- usability

Goal:

Transform the prototype into a coherent product.

---

## 1.0 — Public website

Requirements will be finalized closer to release.

Likely work includes:

- production hosting
- domain
- monitoring
- privacy/security review
- performance testing
- responsive/mobile-browser quality
- accessibility review
- production data reliability
- rollback strategy
- final UX polish

1.0 should represent a website we are comfortable making publicly available.

---

- Android application
